## Executive Summary

The African diaspora flight deal market is a **$2-5B+ annual opportunity** largely ignored by existing players. With 28M+ Africans in the US, UK, and EU sending $100B+ in remittances annually, and flights to Africa costing $1,200-2,000+ roundtrip, there's a clear gap for a diaspora-first flight deal service.

---

## 1. Market Size

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

**African Diaspora Travel Market: ~$5-10B annually**

- **28 million** Africans living in US, UK, and EU ([GFRID](https://gfrid.org/remittances-from-african-diaspora-grew-in-2023-set-to-exceed-100bn-in-2024/))
- **$100B+** in annual remittances to Africa, indicating strong homeland connection ([Afridigest](https://afridigest.com/diaspora-remittances-africa-100-billion-opportunity/))
- Average roundtrip flight: **$1,200-1,800** to West/Central Africa
- If 20% of diaspora travels home annually = 5.6M trips × $1,500 avg = **$8.4B in airfare**

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

**US-based African diaspora: ~$2-3B annually**

- **Nigeria:** 700K-850K total diaspora in US ([Migration Policy Institute](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/sites/default/files/publications/RAD-Nigeria.pdf))
- **Ghana:** 200K+ in US, concentrated in NY, VA, NJ, MD ([Migration Policy Institute](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/sites/default/files/publications/RAD-Ghana.pdf))
- Other West African countries: 300K+ combined
- UK Nigerian diaspora: **270K+** (2021 Census), with 141K Nigerians moving to UK in 2022-23 alone

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

**Year 1 target: $50-100K ARR**

- 1,000-2,000 paying subscribers at $49/year
- Focus: US → Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal routes
- Expansion: UK, Canada origins; East Africa destinations

## 2. Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

| Company | Model | Price | Strengths | Weakness for Diaspora |
|---------|-------|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Going (Scott's Cheap Flights)** | Subscription | $49-199/yr | 2M+ subscribers, $3.8M revenue, avg $550 savings ([Getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/scotts-cheap-flights)) | Africa is afterthought; tourist destinations only |
| **Hopper** | Freemium + fintech | Free app | $850M revenue, 120M+ downloads, price prediction ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/hopper-statistics/)) | US-centric, minimal Africa coverage |
| **Thrifty Traveler** | Subscription | $99/yr | Strong mistake fare coverage ([Thrifty Traveler](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/mistake-fares-guide/)) | No Africa focus |
| **Secret Flying** | Free (affiliate) | Free | Best mistake fare coverage ([Secret Flying](https://www.secretflying.com/errorfare/)) | Manual monitoring required, global not Africa-focused |

### Why They Don't Serve This Market

1. **Volume economics**: Africa routes are low-volume compared to Europe, Asia, Latin America
2. **Complexity**: Multiple carriers, poor data availability, routing complexity
3. **Perceived market**: Viewed as "tourist" market (Cape Town, Marrakech) vs. diaspora cities (Lagos, Accra, Douala)

### Indirect Competitors

- **Google Flights**: Best search, but date-pair locked, no proactive alerts
- **Skyscanner/Kayak**: Aggregators, no personalized monitoring
- **WhatsApp groups**: How deals actually spread in diaspora communities (word of mouth)

## 3. Voice of Customer

### Primary Research: Diaspora Travel Pain Points

**From your background doc + market signals:**

1. **"Destination-locked, date-flexible"**
   - "I need to get to Lagos. I don't care when, I care about price."
   - Current tools assume date flexibility, not destination flexibility

2. **Peak season price gouging**
   - Detty December (Dec-Jan): Flights 2-3x normal prices
   - Airlines know diaspora MUST travel then
   - Delta increased Lagos flights 133% in December ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/travel/detty-december-nigeria-lagos-ghana))

3. **Speed of deals**
   - Mistake fares last minutes to hours
   - By the time you see it on Twitter, it's gone
   - 10-20% of mistake fares get cancelled anyway ([Going](https://www.going.com/guides/mistake-fares))

4. **Trust deficit**
   - "My cousin got Lagos for $600 last month" - word of mouth is primary discovery
   - Skepticism about "deal" sites that push affiliate links

### Secondary Research: Market Signals

**Detty December 2024 Statistics:**
- 1.2M visitors to Lagos, 90% diaspora ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/travel/detty-december-nigeria-lagos-ghana))
- $71.6M generated in Lagos tourism during the season
- Hotels at 90-95% occupancy
- $2,000-3,000 average spend per diaspora visitor

**Flight Pricing Reality:**

## 4. Key Market Insights

### Insight 1: Two Distinct User Segments

You nailed this in your brief:

| Segment | Mindset | Behavior | Tool Needed |
|---------|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Opportunistic Travelers** | "Show me deals, I'll go anywhere" | Flexible on destination AND dates | Deal feed across all Africa destinations |
| **Peak-Time Optimizers** | "I must travel in December" | Fixed on dates, wants best price | Price tracking + booking window alerts |

**Implication**: Product needs to serve both modes, not pick one.

### Insight 2: December is the Money Moment

- 90% of Lagos visitors are diaspora ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/12/19/travel/detty-december-nigeria-party-problems))
- Airlines have perfect pricing power during peak season
- The value proposition for December travelers is different: "When should I book?" not "Where should I go?"

**Implication**: December booking optimization could be a differentiating feature.

### Insight 3: Speed Kills (In a Good Way)

- Mistake fares disappear in minutes/hours
- Existing services (Scott's, Secret Flying) have lag time
- WhatsApp groups are faster but require constant monitoring

**Implication**: Real-time alerting is a competitive moat.

### Insight 4: Trust > Features

- Diaspora communities share deals through trusted networks
- A tool needs to feel like "insider knowledge" not "another travel site"
- Crowdsourced verification ("I just booked this!") builds trust

**Implication**: Community features could accelerate growth.

## 5. Competitive Moat Analysis

### What Would Make Detty Flight Deals Defensible?

| Moat Type | Viability | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Network effects** | Medium | Community deal sharing, crowdsourced verification |
| **Data advantage** | High | Historical pricing for Africa routes (no one has this) |
| **Brand/trust** | High | "By diaspora, for diaspora" authenticity |
| **Speed** | Medium | Real-time monitoring is replicable |
| **Geographic focus** | High | Specialists beat generalists in niche markets |

## 6. Risks and Mitigants

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Going adds Africa focus** | Low | High | Move fast, build community moat |
| **Flight data APIs are expensive/fragile** | High | Medium | Multiple data sources, scraping fallbacks |
| **Airlines reduce mistake fares** | Medium | Medium | Mistake fares are bonus, not core value prop |
| **Low willingness to pay** | Medium | High | Start free, prove value, then monetize |
